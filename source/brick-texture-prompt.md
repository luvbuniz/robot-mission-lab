# Brick baseplate texture

Mode: built-in image generation.

Use case: stylized-concept
Asset type: seamless repeating raster texture for a LEGO-inspired middle-school robotics course interface, used behind the UI and in narrow clipped detail strips.
Primary request: a square 1024x1024 orthographic top-down toy-brick baseplate texture. Evenly spaced identical circular raised studs in a precise square grid covering the entire canvas. Cool neutral pale gray plastic. Subtle realistic lighting and shallow soft shadows give clear but restrained dimensional studs.
Composition/framing: camera exactly perpendicular to the surface, zero perspective or tilt. An 8 by 8 repeating grid of studs, each centered in its grid cell, with spacing at all four edges designed to tile seamlessly. Uniform illumination and color across the whole image, no vignette.
Materials/textures: clean matte molded pale-gray plastic with gently rounded stud edges, smooth tops.
Constraints: perfectly seamless repeat in both directions, no visible plate edge, no border, no background objects, no lettering, logos, embossed writing, watermark, or decorations. No harsh highlights or dramatic shadows. Single texture asset only.

Inspection: neutral gray molded-plastic texture, square 8 by 8 regular stud grid, orthographic view, no text or logos, no border. Generated with seamless repetition requested; exact pixel-perfect edge continuity is not guaranteed by visual inspection.
